Recipe

Classic Pancakes1.

Ingredients.
200 g flour
100 ml milk
2 eggs
2 tomatoes
50 g sugar
50 g beef
10 g butter

Cooking time: 20 minutes

Pre-heat oven to 180 gas mark.

Method.
Take flour from refrigerator.
Put flour into the 1st mixing bowl.
Fold flour into the 1st mixing bowl.
Add dry ingredients to mixing bowl.
Add beef into 1st mixing bowl.
Combine beef from 1st mixing bowl.
Divide beef into 1st mixing bowl.
Remove beef to 1st mixing bowl.
Liquefy contents of the 1st mixing bowl.
Liquefy tomatoes.
Stir the 1st mixing bowl for 2 minutes.
Stir milk into the 2nd mixing bowl.
Mix the 1st mixing bowl well.
Clean 1st mixing bowl.
Pour contents of the 1st mixing bowl into the 1st baking dish.
Refrigerate for 2 hours.
Serve with maple syrup.
Serve the client until night.
Serve the client.

Serves 4.
